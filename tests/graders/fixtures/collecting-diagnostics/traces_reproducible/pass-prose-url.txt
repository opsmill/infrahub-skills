## Plan

Do you want to include traces of the slow page loads? Since you can reproduce it, the window can be
the next 30 minutes.

Only a share of requests has a trace. Before you reproduce the slowness, a super admin sends
PUT `https://infrahub.example.com:8000/api/troubleshooting/trace-sample-ratio` with the body
`{"ratio": 1.0, "duration_seconds": 1800}`.

Open the page three times, then create the bundle after your yes:

```bash
infrahub-collect create --include-traces --traces-min-duration 1s --traces-since 30m
```

After the bundle is created, the super admin sends
DELETE `https://infrahub.example.com:8000/api/troubleshooting/trace-sample-ratio`.
