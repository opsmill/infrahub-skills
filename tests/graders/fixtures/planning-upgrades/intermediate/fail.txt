# Upgrade plan: 1.5.2 to 1.10.0

## 1.5 -> 1.6

| Change | Release | Kind | Severity | When | Affected | Evidence | Action | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| node_metadata is now a reserved attribute name | 1.10.0 | breaking | critical | before | yes | schemas/circuit.yml -> InfraCircuit.node_metadata | Rename the attribute before upgrading; Infrahub refuses to load the schema otherwise | https://github.com/opsmill/infrahub/releases/tag/infrahub-v1.10.0 |

```bash
# read-only: reports what the upgrade would do, writes nothing
docker compose exec infrahub-server infrahub upgrade --check
```
