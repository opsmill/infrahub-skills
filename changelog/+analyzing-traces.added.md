The Diagnostics Analyzer skill reads the traces of a bundle collected with `infrahub-collect create --include-traces`. It reads the `traces` entry of `bundle_information.json`, then each file of `bundle/traces/` from the slowest, and reports the service and the operation that took the time, from the duration and the self time of each span. It starts and queries no Tempo or Grafana. For a slowness without usable traces, the report asks for a next bundle with `--include-traces` and a window that covers the slowness.
